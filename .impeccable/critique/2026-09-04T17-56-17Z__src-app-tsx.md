## Priority Issues

[P1] Audit trail can misattribute who actually resolved an incident
- Where: IncidentDesk.tsx:185, handlePanelResolve uses live.assigneeName ?? live.reporterName instead of currentUser.name (every other handler is correct).
- Why it matters: verified live - resolving an incident assigned to T. Alvarez while acting as H. Scaife logs "RESOLVED T. Alvarez." Undercuts PRODUCT.md's immutable-audit-trail claim.
- Fix: one-line change to actorName: currentUser.name.

[P1] Two frequently-used controls violate the product's own 44px touch-target minimum
- Where: panel CLOSE button and header role-switcher, confirmed missing min-h-11.
- Why it matters: PRODUCT.md names 44px explicitly; these are two of the most-used controls.
- Fix: add min-h-11 and matching padding.

[P1] Detail panel's ARIA role and desktop positioning are both slightly wrong
- Where: IncidentDetailPanel.tsx. role="dialog" declared but focus isn't trapped (deliberate non-modal design), so the role itself is the wrong semantic choice. Panel spans full viewport height on desktop, covering header chrome while open.
- Why it matters: screen-reader gets a dialog announcement that doesn't behave like one; sighted users lose the live clock exactly when needed most.
- Fix: swap to a non-dialog role (region + aria-label), offset panel below header instead of top-0.

[P2] Large unexplained dead space in the detail panel
- Why it matters: AuditTrailFeed pinned via mt-auto leaves ~350-380px empty for incidents without active mitigation.
- Suggested command: /impeccable layout

[P3] No search/filter/sort, no keyboard shortcuts
- Why it matters: feed is scroll-and-click only, straining "speed over completeness" as volume grows.
- Suggested command: /impeccable shape
